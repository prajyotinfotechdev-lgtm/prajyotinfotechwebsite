# OIBUZ

## Construction Workforce & Business Management Platform

### 1. Executive Summary

Oibuz is a comprehensive workforce and business management platform designed to help companies streamline their daily operations. For construction companies, managing a distributed workforce across multiple sites introduces significant challenges: tracking accurate attendance, managing leave requests, monitoring project hours, processing expense reimbursements, and running error-free payroll.

Oibuz solves these challenges by providing a centralized, role-based system. Employees can punch in from their phones with photo and location verification. Managers can instantly review and approve team timesheets and expenses. HR teams gain complete visibility over the entire workforce, and Finance can securely process reimbursements. By replacing scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and paper forms with structured digital workflows, Oibuz gives business owners total control and visibility over their workforce operations and costs.

### 2. What is Oibuz?

Oibuz is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform built to handle the complete employee lifecycle and daily operational administration. It acts as the central nervous system for your company's workforce. 

Rather than relying on disjointed systems—one for attendance, another for payroll, and paper trails for expenses—Oibuz brings everything into a single, unified environment. It is designed to be accessible from anywhere, ensuring that site workers, office staff, and executives are all working from the same real-time information.

### 3. Who Oibuz is Designed For

While Oibuz is versatile, it provides exceptional value to **Construction Companies, Contractors, and Project-Based Businesses**. 

In construction, the workforce is rarely sitting in front of a computer. Staff are distributed across active building sites, project managers are constantly on the move, and the back office (HR and Finance) needs a reliable way to collect data from the field without chasing down supervisors. Oibuz is built for this exact environment, providing simple field-level data entry with robust management oversight.

### 4. Problems Oibuz Addresses

*   **Manual & Unverified Attendance:**
    *   *Problem:* Workers calling in or signing paper registers makes it difficult to verify if they were actually on site and on time.
    *   *Solution:* Oibuz allows mobile punch-ins requiring a selfie and capturing the exact location.
    *   *Benefit:* Eliminates "buddy punching" and ensures you only pay for actual time on site.
*   **Unstructured Approvals & Lost Requests:**
    *   *Problem:* Leave requests and expense claims get lost in WhatsApp chats or verbal agreements, causing payroll delays and disputes.
    *   *Solution:* Oibuz routes all requests through a strict, trackable approval hierarchy.
    *   *Benefit:* Clear accountability. Nothing gets lost, and every approval has a complete audit trail.
*   **Disconnected Payroll Administration:**
    *   *Problem:* Calculating payroll requires manually combining attendance records, approved leaves, and timesheets at the end of every month.
    *   *Solution:* Oibuz automatically calculates payroll based on the system's attendance and leave data.
    *   *Benefit:* Cuts payroll processing time from days to minutes and eliminates human calculation errors.
*   **Lack of Management Visibility:**
    *   *Problem:* Business owners struggle to get a clear picture of workforce costs, team sizes, and pending operational bottlenecks.
    *   *Solution:* Real-time dashboards and automated SLA escalations (alerts when managers take too long to approve items).
    *   *Benefit:* Proactive management rather than reactive firefighting.

### 5. How Oibuz Works

Oibuz operates on a strict **Role-Based Hierarchy**. Information flows sequentially from the people on the ground up to management and the back office.

When an employee submits data (like a timesheet or an expense receipt), it does not instantly become approved. It routes to their direct Manager. The Manager reviews the request against project reality. Once the Manager approves, it flows to HR (for policy verification) or Finance (for payment processing). This ensures that no single person can bypass company controls.

### 6. User Roles

#### 6.1 Employee
The foundation of the system. Employees use Oibuz to manage their daily work requirements. They can punch in/out, log hours against specific projects/tasks, request time off, submit expense receipts for reimbursement, and download their monthly salary slips. They only ever see their own personal data.

#### 6.2 Manager
Managers are responsible for operational oversight. A Manager has access to all Employee features, plus a "Team View." They can see the attendance records of their direct reports, review team leave balances, and are the first line of approval for timesheets, leaves, and reimbursements. Managers cannot see the data of employees who do not report to them.

#### 6.3 HR
The Human Resources team has tenant-wide visibility. They add new employees, assign them to managers, and maintain official company profiles. HR oversees the entire leave and attendance landscape, stepping in to approve requests after managers, handling attendance corrections, and running the monthly payroll calculations.

#### 6.4 Finance
The Finance role is specialized for financial control. In the current platform, Finance acts as the final gatekeeper for Employee Reimbursements. After a Manager validates an expense and HR approves it according to policy, Finance reviews the receipts and issues final clearance for payment.

#### 6.5 Admin
The Admin is the highest level of control, typically held by business owners or system directors. Admins can view all data across the company, configure system-wide policies, review comprehensive audit logs (to see exactly who did what), and override workflows if a manager is unavailable. 

### 7. Core Modules

#### 7.1 Employee Management
A centralized digital directory of all staff. HR can create profiles, define job roles, set base salary details, assign reporting managers, and deactivate departing employees securely.

#### 7.2 Attendance Management
A robust tracking system where employees punch in and out. It captures the exact time, the employee's location, and a selfie photo. It also includes an "Attendance Correction" workflow, allowing employees to request a fix if they forget to punch out, which managers can then approve.

#### 7.3 Leave Management
Tracks employee time off. Employees can view their available balances and submit requests. The system automatically prevents users from applying for more leave than they have accrued. Interestingly, if an employee punches in for work on a day they had a pending leave request, Oibuz automatically cancels the leave.

#### 7.4 Timesheet Management
Allows employees to log the exact hours spent on specific projects and tasks daily. Managers can review these logs to understand where labor hours are being spent across different construction sites.

#### 7.5 Reimbursement Management
A digital expense claim system. Employees upload photos of physical receipts (e.g., site materials, travel). The claim moves through a multi-level approval process, ensuring Finance only pays for verified, manager-approved business expenses.

#### 7.6 Payroll Management
HR uses this module at the end of the month to generate a "Payroll Run." The system aggregates the base salary data, cross-references it with attendance and approved leaves, and calculates the final payable amounts for the entire workforce.

#### 7.7 Salary Slips
Once HR finalizes and publishes the payroll, Oibuz automatically generates professional, standardized PDF salary slips. Employees can securely download their own slips directly from their dashboard without needing to email HR.

#### 7.8 Approval & Escalation Management
All requests (Leaves, Timesheets, Reimbursements) utilize a unified approval engine. Furthermore, Oibuz includes an SLA (Service Level Agreement) Escalation feature. If a manager ignores a pending request for too long, the system flags it and escalates it, ensuring operational bottlenecks are broken.

#### 7.9 Reports & Management Visibility
Provides consolidated views for leadership, including daily/monthly attendance summaries, team weekly productivity reports, and workforce demographic/cost summaries.

#### 7.10 Other Available Modules
*   **Support Tickets:** An internal helpdesk allowing employees to raise issues or HR inquiries directly within the platform.
*   **Audit Logging:** An invisible, unalterable background log that records critical actions (who approved what, who changed a salary, etc.) for ultimate accountability.

### 8. End-to-End Business Workflows

**The Leave Request Journey:**
1.  **Employee** checks their balance and submits a leave request.
2.  **Manager** receives the request, reviews site coverage, and Approves.
3.  **HR** receives the manager-approved request, verifies company policy, and gives Final Approval.
4.  The system deducts the balance.

**The Reimbursement Journey:**
1.  **Employee** buys site supplies, snaps a photo of the receipt, and submits a claim.
2.  **Manager** verifies the supplies were actually needed for the project and Approves.
3.  **HR** verifies the expense aligns with general company policies.
4.  **Finance** receives the fully vetted claim, verifies the receipt mathematics, Approves, and processes the cash payout.

**The Payroll Journey:**
1.  Month ends. **HR** initiates a new Payroll Run.
2.  Oibuz automatically pulls all Employee salaries, approved leaves, and attendance records.
3.  **HR** calculates the run and reviews the numbers.
4.  **HR** finalizes and publishes the run.
5.  **Employees** instantly receive access to their generated PDF payslips.

### 9. Construction Company Use Case

Imagine **Apex Builders**, a construction firm with 3 active sites, 50 site workers, 3 site managers, an HR officer, and a Finance lead at headquarters.

*   **Morning:** At 8:00 AM, site workers arrive at their respective sites. They open Oibuz on their phones, take a selfie, and punch in. Oibuz logs their location, proving they are actually on site, not at home.
*   **Mid-Day:** A site manager needs to buy emergency cement. He pays out of pocket, takes a photo of the receipt, and submits a reimbursement request. 
*   **Afternoon:** A worker realizes they forgot to punch out yesterday. They submit an "Attendance Correction" request. The site manager reviews it, adjusts the time to 5:00 PM, and approves it.
*   **End of Week:** Site workers submit their timesheets, logging 20 hours to "Site A Foundation" and 20 hours to "Site B Framing." The site managers approve them.
*   **End of Month:** The HR officer at headquarters clicks "Create Payroll." Oibuz automatically calculates deductions for the workers who took unpaid leave and compiles the data. The Finance lead reviews the approved emergency cement receipt and issues the refund. Employees download their payslips securely. 

Everything happens without a single piece of paper traveling between the sites and headquarters.

### 12. Business Benefits

*   **Total Accountability:** With selfies, location tracking, and unalterable audit logs, you know exactly who was where, and who approved what.
*   **Elimination of Paperwork:** Stop losing receipts, paper leave forms, and manual timesheets between the job site and the head office.
*   **Faster Payroll Processing:** By centralizing attendance and leaves, month-end payroll calculations take minutes rather than days.
*   **Structured Control:** Multi-level approvals ensure that money (reimbursements) and time (leaves) cannot be approved without proper management oversight.
*   **Centralized Source of Truth:** If a manager leaves the company, their team's data, history, and pending approvals remain safely in the system, rather than disappearing in a personal WhatsApp history.

### 13. Why Oibuz for Construction Companies?

Oibuz is highly relevant to construction companies because it inherently understands **distributed workforces**. 

In an office environment, a manager can look across the room to see who is present. In construction, a project manager might be responsible for staff across three different building sites. Oibuz's location-verified attendance bridges that physical gap. Furthermore, the strict Manager → HR workflow ensures that the people closest to the actual work (Site Managers) validate data before the back office (HR/Finance) processes it, completely eliminating the disconnect between the field and headquarters.

### 14. Current Product Capabilities

The capabilities detailed in this report represent the **active, functional modules** currently available within the Oibuz platform. The system successfully handles the core lifecycle of employee data, time tracking, basic project logging, expense routing, and payroll generation natively.

### 15. Potential Future Capabilities

While Oibuz is powerful, it is important to distinguish current features from future roadmap items. The following features are common in the industry but are **not currently implemented** in Oibuz:
*   *Hardware Biometric Integration* (Currently relies on mobile selfie/GPS).
*   *Continuous GPS Tracking* (Currently only captures location at the exact moment of punch in/out).
*   *Advanced Project Accounting & Job Costing* (Currently tracks hours per project, but does not calculate heavy project profitability).
*   *Automated Tax & GST Filing* (Payroll calculates gross/net, but does not act as tax software).

### 16. Final Product Overview

Oibuz is a mature, highly structured operational platform. It replaces chaos with process. For a construction firm looking to modernize its workforce management, Oibuz delivers immediate value by locking down attendance, digitizing expenses, and automating payroll administration—all wrapped in a secure, role-based hierarchy that keeps business owners in total control.
